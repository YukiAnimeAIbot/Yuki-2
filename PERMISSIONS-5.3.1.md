# Active Permission Model

- Owner identity is loaded from the configured owner environment variables and owner store.
- Group-setting and moderation actions require the Owner or an actual WhatsApp group admin as appropriate.
- Global bot-ban commands are Owner-only after the staff management system was removed.
- Global Mod/Guidance role management and its Supabase integration are no longer active.
- The bot's AI/group middleware and command pacing remain active.
